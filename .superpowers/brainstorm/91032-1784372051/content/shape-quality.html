Steve Jobs' Take on Flameshot Shapes

"The smallest of details matter. The line endings. The curve of an arrow. The way a shape sits on the page."

Current Problems

Sharp Line Endings

All lines use SquareCap and BevelJoin — sharp, ugly corners. Apple uses RoundCap/RoundJoin everywhere. A line with SquareCap extends past its endpoint by half the width. Rounded caps feel intentional, finished.

Clipped Arrowheads

The arrow shaft is shortened by its thickness so it doesn't poke through the head. But this creates a visible gap. Apple would draw the head smoothly overlapping the shaft with no gap.

Rectangle Fills Instead of Strokes

When you set thickness > 0 on a rectangle, it fills with rounded corners — it doesn't stroke the outline. This means you can't draw a thin outlined rectangle. Apple would separate stroke vs fill.

Flat Shapes, No Depth

No shadows, no subtle glow, no sense of material. Everything sits flat on the image. Apple would add a whisper of a shadow to make shapes feel deliberate, like they sit on top of the image.

Jagged Pencil Paths

Pencil tool uses raw drawPolyline() — every mouse movement becomes a straight segment. No smoothing. Apple would apply bezier curve fitting for a smooth, fluid stroke.

What Would Apple Do?

Tool Change
All tools RoundCap + RoundJoin on every line stroke. Subtle drop shadow on shapes.
Arrow Redrawn arrowhead with smooth curves (bezier), no gap with shaft. Think Photos app markup.
Line Rounded caps + join. Option to make it a filled pill shape instead of just a stroke.
Rectangle Render as stroked outline (with thickness) regardless of size. Rounded corners always on.
Circle Rounded strokes. Optional subtle fill at lower opacity.
Pencil Bezier curve fitting — smooth, elegant lines instead of jagged polylines.
CircleCount Already the most polished tool. Keep as-is, just add rounded strokes.
Marker Softer edges — use QPainterPath with rounded cap/join on the highlight.
